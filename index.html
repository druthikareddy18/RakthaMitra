Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using RakthaMitra.

Does a donor response confirm donation?

No. A response only means the donor is willing to be contacted. It does not confirm eligibility, compatibility, or that a donation has taken place.

Can I use RakthaMitra for urgent needs?

For urgent blood needs, contact your hospital and an authorized blood bank immediately. Do not rely only on website listings or notifications.

How do I keep my donor listing updated?

Sign in, open My Profile, and update your contact details and availability.

Can I track a request later?

Yes. Sign in and open Request History to review your requests and their latest recorded status.

Help & support

Need a hand using RakthaMitra?

For login, profile, donor-search or request-tracking issues, first check the FAQs above. If the issue continues, contact the RakthaMitra project team or your college project supervisor and include a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords or private medical documents.

RakthaMitra coordinates information and responses only. For urgent needs, contact your hospital or an authorized blood bank. Donor availability, eligibility, compatibility, and any donation must be confirmed by qualified healthcare professionals.
